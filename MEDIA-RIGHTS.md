# Media rights and attribution

English · [简体中文](#简体中文)

Videos, prompts, quoted posts, music, brands and depicted assets belong to their respective rights holders. Listing them here does not transfer ownership and does not make them MIT-licensed or free to reuse commercially.

This repository does not download or re-host videos. Previews are the thumbnails X serves for each post, and every entry links to the creator's original post. Model attribution follows the creator's own statement; we have no access to their generation logs.

To request a correction, a different credit or removal, [open an issue](https://github.com/sandbase-lab/awesome-gemini4-argon/issues/new) with the post URL and the change you need. Removals are recorded in `data/collection.json` so the entry is not re-added.

This collection is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. SandBase links are added by the maintainers and do not imply creator endorsement.
